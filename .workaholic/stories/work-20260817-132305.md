## 2. Motivation

A ticket written from a bad metric is worse than no ticket: it names files, ranks them, and invites work.
This one claimed 18 affected pages and inferred a structural split between hand-written and generated
pages — all from counting backticks in rendered HTML outside `<code>`, a measure that also counts
attributes and comments. Its own step 2 said to establish the mechanism *before editing any prose*, and
that instruction is what caught it one ticket later. Correcting the record — including withdrawing the
concern the previous branch's story carried, so it is never extracted into the feedback stream as real —
is the whole deliverable.
